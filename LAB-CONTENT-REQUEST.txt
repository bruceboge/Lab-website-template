DICEL WEBSITE CONTENT REQUEST
==========================================

Purpose
-------
Please provide approved, current information for the website. Some names,
statistics, project descriptions, schedules, equipment entries, and course
details currently shown are sample content only. They must be checked and
replaced before the website is published. If a detail is not approved for
public use, mark it "do not publish" rather than supplying private information.

1. Official lab identity and overview
-------------------------------------
- Official public name of the lab and approved acronym.
- Approved description of DICEL's relationship to Dedan Kimathi University of
  Technology.
- Approved short description (1-2 sentences) for the homepage and search
  results.
- Approved overview, purpose, mission, vision, and history/milestones, with
  dates that can be verified.
- Approved research and teaching areas, in priority order.
- Preferred public terminology and any wording that should not be used.

2. Research projects
-------------------
For each project the lab wants listed:
- Approved title, short summary, and public project description.
- Current status and the date that status was last checked.
- Project lead and contributors, with approved public names and roles.
- Research question, approach, and results that may be stated publicly.
- Exact measurements, units, test conditions, and evidence supporting each
  numerical result or performance claim.
- Process node, platform, tools, partners, funding acknowledgements, and
  intellectual-property or release restrictions, where applicable.
- Public report, repository, presentation, or publication links.
- Approved images, captions, and image credits.

3. Staff and student profiles
-----------------------------
For every person to be listed:
- Name as they wish it to appear, current official role, and affiliation.
- Approved short biography and research interests.
- University email or other contact route approved for publication.
- Optional portrait and written permission to publish it.
- Confirmation of whether student profiles and contact details may be shown.

4. Courses and learning material
--------------------------------
- Official course title and code, programme, level/year, and semester.
- Approved course summary, prerequisites, learning outcomes, and syllabus.
- Verified lecture/lab hours, duration, assessment details, and required
  equipment or software.
- Instructor name and public contact route, if approved.
- Official course outline or learning-resource links.
- Confirmation of which courses and learning pathways are currently offered.

5. Equipment, facilities, and borrowing
---------------------------------------
- Current equipment name, manufacturer, model, and confirmed quantity.
- Number available for loan versus equipment restricted to supervised lab use.
- Availability source and update frequency; do not describe stock as real-time
  unless it is actually maintained in real time.
- Eligibility, required approvals, loan duration, collection/return process,
  safety conditions, and contact for assistance.
- Confirmed facility names, location/building/room, opening hours, access
  requirements, and accessibility information.
- Approved photographs of the actual spaces and equipment, with captions,
  credits, and permission to publish. Identify any illustration as an
  illustration rather than a photograph of the facility.

6. Events
---------
For each current or archived event:
- Approved title, status, date, start/end time, and time zone.
- Confirmed venue and accessibility or attendance requirements.
- Approved summary, agenda, speakers, affiliations, and speaker consent.
- Registration link, capacity, fees (if any), and organizer contact.
- For past events, confirmed outcomes and approved photos/captions.
- Date on which the listing should be reviewed or removed.

7. Publications and research outputs
------------------------------------
For each output:
- Full citation exactly as it should appear, including author order.
- Publication type, venue/publisher, year, volume/issue/pages where available.
- DOI or stable public URL and a link to the permitted full text.
- Publication status (published, accepted, preprint, report, or other).
- Confirmation from the authors that the record and link are correct.
- For software, datasets, or hardware, the public release link and license.

8. Contact and institutional details
------------------------------------
- Public email, telephone, postal/physical address, and map link.
- Confirmed visitor and office hours, including holiday/appointment caveats.
- Correct university URLs, social accounts, and preferred contact
  route for students, partners, equipment requests, and media enquiries.
- Approved privacy, accessibility, and website feedback contacts or policies.

9. Review and approval
---------------------
- Name/role of the person authorized to approve public website content.
- For each section above, mark: approved, needs correction, or do not publish.
- Include the date of approval and the next review date for time-sensitive
  information such as people, inventory, events, and project status.
- Flag claims requiring legal, university communications, funder, partner, or
  participant approval before publication.
